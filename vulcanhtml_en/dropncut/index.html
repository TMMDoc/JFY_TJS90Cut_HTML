SmartRemnant

SmartRemnant

The SmartRemnant feature uses a network camera mounted inside the machine to provide a live view of the sheet on the pallet. After calibrating the camera, you can place parts directly onto the camera image in postproduction, and TJS Cut-90 automatically captures the work offset — eliminating the need for manual offset entry.

Steps to calibrate

  1. Go to Configure → Calibration.

    ① Shows live position diode coordinates.
    ② Status message to guide the user through the calibration process.
    ③ Displays the saved bound region.
    ④ To capture a new bound region.

  2. The live image from the camera will appear on the control screen.

  3. Click Show Bound to view the saved calibration boundary. This helps to verify the visible area and confirm whether the camera is correctly positioned to capture the desired portion of the sheet.

  4. If the displayed boundary does not match the desired sheet area, click Capture to reset and provide new bound points.

  5. Proceed with calibration by measuring four reference points one by one on the sheet by guiding the position diode and clicking on it, thus forming an exact rectangular boundary.

Capture mode

① Guides the order of points to be captured.
② To exit capture mode without saving changes.

Once the position diode is placed at the required position and a point is clicked on the position diode, the position diode position will automatically be captured for the corresponding point.

If the position diode point is not updating when the laser head is moved in the calibration page, the textbox prompts for user-input of the machine point which must be entered manually and confirmed by pressing Enter or clicking outside the textbox.

Once all four points are given, a confirmation popup appears. If confirmed, the bound will be added; if not, the previous bound will be retained. After confirmation, the bound will be displayed.

Calibration is a one-time process that remains valid unless the camera position or pallet changes.

Using the calibrated view in SmartRemnant

  1. Once the bound is saved, head to SmartRemnant by choosing the desired LST file from Programs.

  2. The camera view in SmartRemnant takes up the saved bound, within which parts can be added and saved.

  3. Click Save NC — the work offset is captured with respect to the bound region. The saved part can then be cut without needing any manual capture of work offset.
