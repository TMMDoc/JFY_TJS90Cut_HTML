Sample Program with Multiple Parts

The below program is designed with 3 different parts, with certain contours having marking definitions etc., for the reference:

    %1 (PP_Sample_Pgm.DIN)
    Sheet_Size_X = 120
    Sheet_Size_Y = 600
    Job_Size_X = 105
    Job_Size_Y = 573
    X_Limit_Minus = 10
    X_Limit_Plus = 115
    Y_Limit_Minus = 10
    Y_Limit_Plus = 583
    G90
    M15
    G17 D=SheetRotation
    G253 F="TT:MS_1mm"
    H0

    BlockNo=1
